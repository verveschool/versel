An objection is not an obstacle. It is a signal. Objections are logic problems: price, curriculum, time, or fit. Stalls are fear problems: let me think about it, send information, I need to talk to someone.

Objections are loopable because they are about specifics. Stalls are not solved by more pitching. When someone stalls, return to certainty. Remind them of their goal, the path you discussed, and ask what happens if they wait six months. Then ask whether it makes more sense to start now or keep waiting.
