Tonality creates psychological effects. Use the reasonable man when making an ask: conversational, relaxed, and matter-of-fact. Use absolute certainty when talking about the product: firm, direct, and without hedging. Use the scarcity whisper in the close: quieter, slower, and intimate.

Practice recording yourself. Practice the transitions. When tones become automatic, they become part of how you breathe on a call.
