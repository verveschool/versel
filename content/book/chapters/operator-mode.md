Persuasion is a skill. It is built by understanding how people make decisions and building the certainties that move them to action.

Operators record calls, ask for feedback, study wins, study losses, and chase the next small improvement. They care about the person. They create value in a conversation instead of extracting it.

The book is a map. The work is on the phone, in the conversation, and in caring enough to get good.
