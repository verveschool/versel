Real trust is not likeability. Real trust is when someone believes you care whether this is the right decision for them more than you care about closing.

The signals are competence, listening, upfront limitations, willingness to say no, alignment between words and actions, and genuine curiosity. Smart questions create respect. Follow-ups prove you are listening. Honest limits prove you care about outcomes.

The thing that separates great closers is integrity. They do not want to sell to someone who would make a bad decision.
