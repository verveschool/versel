Most salespeople ask bad questions. Good questions move the conversation forward. Start with consultative questions to understand what is going on beneath the surface. Move from broad to specific to emotional to honest.

Then use pattern questions to reveal how they think about the problem and what beliefs are stopping them. If they say they are not smart enough, ask where that belief comes from and whether it has actually been tested.

Near the close, use confirmation questions: does this make sense, and if it worked this way, would it change things? The progression matters: consult to pattern to confirmation.
