The loop is not repeating your pitch. It is a sequence: deflect and pace, isolate, build airtight logic, bridge to emotion, stack emotional benefits, and ask again.

Acknowledge the concern first. Then isolate whether the core idea makes sense. Stack factual reasons the objection does not block the solution. Use a bridge like “moreover” or “but beyond that” to move from logic to feeling. Then paint what they get emotionally and ask for the next action.
