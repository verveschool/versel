This is the VerveSchool standard. We do not accept excuses. We accept explanations, learn from failure, and refuse mediocrity.

Operators protect each other, push each other, celebrate wins, learn from losses, and hold the standard when it is uncomfortable. You will record calls, listen to them, get feedback, give feedback, and care about outcomes.

The world moves for those who do. And do not you dare settle for fine.
