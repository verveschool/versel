Once they acknowledge the problem, do not rush into the solution. Box them in. Make them admit their current alternatives do not work.

Ask what they have tried and what happened. Test the free route, the long formal route, the solo route, and the do-nothing route. If free learning would work, would they not have done it already? If formal education is the path, do they have the time and money? If solo work is enough, do they have the accountability? If nothing changes, where are they in one year?

This is consulting through elimination. You are not forcing a purchase. You are making reality visible.
