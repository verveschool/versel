This is the move most salespeople miss.

Once you have figured out the current state and they have acknowledged the problem, most salespeople move immediately to presenting the solution.

Amateur move.

What you do instead is box them in. You make them admit that their current alternatives do not work.

You methodically destroy their backup plans so they have to admit that something has to change and it has to be serious.

This does two things at once.

It proves the problem is painful and unsolvable with what they have been trying. And it creates pressure for them to consider taking action instead of staying stuck.

Here is how you do it.

Start with what they have tried.

"You mentioned you have been looking for better opportunities. What have you done?"

They tell you.

"And what happened?"

They answer.

"So that approach did not work."

Now box them in on their other options.

Option one is the free route. YouTube, self teaching, online resources.

"You could do what most people do. Spend six months or a year watching YouTube videos and teaching yourself. No money out of pocket. The question is, have you done that? And if you did, what happened? Did you finish? Did it lead to a job?"

They usually say no or they are still not sure.

Then you go for it.

"So you have been thinking about this for how long? And you have not pulled the trigger on free learning yet. Why do you think that is? And more importantly, if free learning was going to work for you, would you not have done it already?"

This is not mean. It is accurate. You are holding up a mirror to their inaction.

Option two is the long road. College, degree, formal program.

"Another path is the traditional route. Go back to school. Two years minimum. Ten to fifteen lakhs. You could do that. Do you have two years? Do you have that money right now? And are you ready to go back to being a student?"

They realize they are not. The pieces do not fit.

Option three is solo. Figuring it out on your own.

"You could also try to figure this out completely solo. Learn by doing. Find a job and fumble your way through. The question is, do you think that is going to work? Are you someone who can force yourself to learn hard things when nobody is holding you accountable? Most people cannot. Most people need structure and someone who has been through it."

Option four is staying where you are.

"Or you could do nothing. Keep your current job, keep making what you are making, keep feeling how you feel. Stay stuck. What happens in six months? What happens in a year? Where are you in three years if nothing changes?"

Watch what happens.

You have systematically eliminated every alternative.

Free learning will not work because they would have done it. Formal education takes too long and costs too much. Solo requires discipline they probably do not have. Staying the same is actively harmful.

Suddenly the picture is clear. The only viable option is something structured and guided. Something like what you are offering.

This is consulting through elimination.

You are showing them what is possible given their constraints. You are not suggesting they buy. You are suggesting they face reality.

Once they have admitted their alternatives do not work, when you present your solution, they are primed. They are ready. They have already eliminated the other options.
