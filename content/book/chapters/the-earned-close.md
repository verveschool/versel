A close is not an event. It is an accumulation.

You build all the certainties perfectly and then most salespeople blow it at the last moment because they do not know how to ask for the money.

Here is why closes fail.

Asking too soon.

You ask for the sale before they are ready. You have figured out the problem but you have not amplified it enough. You have mentioned the solution but you have not made the case for why they specifically must buy it.

Asking the wrong question.

Instead of asking for commitment, you ask a question that invites negotiation.

"So what do you think?" or "Does that sound interesting?" or "Would you want to move forward?"

All of these are soft. All of these invite objections.

Ask for action instead.

Asking like you are unsure.

You end the sentence with uptalk. You use qualifiers. "If you want to" or "if you think it makes sense."

You sound like you are hoping. You sound desperate.

Asking and then talking.

You ask them "do we get started?" and then before they can answer, you keep talking.

Silence after a close is sacred. You ask. You wait. You do not interrupt.

Not asking at all.

You spend an hour building perfect certainty and then at the end you say, "okay, well let me send you more information and you can think about it."

You do not ask. You do not commit. You leave them on the hook indefinitely.

What a clean close sounds like:

"Based on everything we have talked about, I think this is the right move for you. And I want to work with you. So here is what needs to happen. You are going to come in next Monday. We will run through onboarding. You will meet the team. We will get started the following week. What does that schedule look like for you?"

Notice what you have done.

You have stated that it is the right move. You have stated that you want to work together. You have told them what is going to happen.

Then you asked a logistical question, not a commitment question.

When they start answering the logistical question, they have answered the real question. They have committed.

If they object again, loop once. Address the objection with the mechanical loop. Ask again.

If they object again, tell them the truth.

"I get it. And I respect that. But here is what I also know. You came to me because something needs to change. You said you cannot keep doing what you are doing. You said the situation is costing you. All of that is still true right now."

If they still say no, let them go. Do not chase. Do not negotiate down. Do not try another angle.

The close with integrity is the hardest close because it requires that you do not need the sale.
