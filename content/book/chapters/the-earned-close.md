A close is not an event. It is an accumulation. Closes fail when you ask too soon, ask weak questions, sound unsure, talk after asking, or avoid asking at all.

A clean close states your recommendation, names the next step, and asks a logistical question. Then you wait. Silence after a close is sacred. If they object, loop once. If they still resist, tell the truth without chasing. Integrity requires not needing the sale.
