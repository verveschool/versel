Great closers have speed of diagnosis. They recognize patterns. They know whether someone is stuck and wants out, complacent, greedy for upside, or skeptical and evidence-hungry.

Build this by recording calls, reviewing wins and losses, noticing what questions work with which profiles, and getting feedback from people better than you. Over time, conscious competence becomes unconscious competence.
