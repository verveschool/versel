You become dangerous when the frameworks are automatic. You stop selling and start diagnosing. You recognize quality immediately. You move faster because you are not thinking through every step. You close more because you are not desperate.

Mastery is learn, apply, bomb, get feedback, adjust, and repeat until the work becomes invisible.
