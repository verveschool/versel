Before you close, paint the future. Return to the gap they identified, name the current state, then describe the future state with detail. Help them imagine the role, the income, the conversations, the confidence, and the respect.

When they can see themselves as the transformed person, they are not deciding whether to buy. They are deciding whether to move toward that version of themselves.
