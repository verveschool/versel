Price is what you pay once. Cost is what you pay over time for making a bad choice. Reframe the calculation.

Use their exact numbers. If they earn thirty thousand and the role creates eighty thousand, the difference is fifty thousand every month. Waiting four months costs two lakh. That means doing nothing can cost the entire program price while leaving them with nothing to show for it.

The cost of waiting is not zero.
