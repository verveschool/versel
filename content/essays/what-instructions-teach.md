Read the last instruction you gave someone.

It taught more than the task itself.

Every instruction teaches something.

The question is what.

When you tell someone exactly what to do, the work gets done.

The thinking stays with you.

When you explain why, the work still gets done.

The thinking starts to spread.

That difference compounds.

People stop waiting for permission.

They make better decisions without asking.

They catch mistakes before you do.

Good teams aren't built by giving fewer instructions.

They're built by giving better ones.

Every instruction is an investment.

You can invest in compliance.

Or you can invest in judgment.

The first scales today's work.

The second scales tomorrow's team.

Read your last message again.

It will tell you what you were teaching.
