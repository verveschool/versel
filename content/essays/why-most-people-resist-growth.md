Most people do not want growth. They want permission to stay stuck.

I learned this the hard way. For a long time, I thought my role was to push. Help people move.

But most people are not stuck because they cannot move. They are stuck because they do not want to.

It shows up in two ways:

- I am not good enough yet.
- This work is beneath me.

Different words. Different insecurities. Same ending.

The hard part: You cannot help someone who has no point to prove. You can share advice. Tools. Time. Chances. It does not matter.

Until they decide, everything sounds good and changes nothing.

So if you are leading a team or building something, stop pushing people who have not chosen to move. If they want it, help them. If they do not, step back.

Lesson I learned as an edtech founder: Effort gets attention. Decision creates progress.

Without decision, effort is false hope. Save your energy. Spend it on people who have already decided.
