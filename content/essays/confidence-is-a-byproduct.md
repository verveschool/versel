Confidence is a byproduct.

What matters is what comes before.

The questions you can't answer.

The skill you don't have.

The part of yourself that feels unfinished.

Most people move on.

Not because it's difficult.

Because it's uncomfortable to stay where you don't understand.

A few people don't.

They keep coming back.

They read one more book.

Try one more approach.

Ask one more question.

Slowly, confusion turns into understanding.

Understanding turns into ability.

Ability becomes confidence.

Your greatest strengths rarely begin as strengths.

They begin as things you cared about enough not to abandon.

Confidence comes later.

Not because you chased it.

Because you stayed with uncertainty long enough for it to teach you.
